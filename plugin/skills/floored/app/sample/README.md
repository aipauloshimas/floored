# Sample take

`source.mp4` is an AI-generated video (made with Seedance 2.0) of a person who doesn't exist, standing on grass and
filmed from above. It is included so you can try floored without filming anything.

The other files are derived from it by floored's own steps: the cutout (`person.webm`), its shadow (`shadow.webm`), the
voice (`audio.m4a`), the picture on the home screen (`poster.jpg`) and the measurements (`take.json`).
