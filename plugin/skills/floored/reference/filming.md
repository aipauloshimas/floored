# How to film it

Show this to the creator before they record, or when a take has warnings. Plain words, second person, no jargon.

- **Phone up high, looking down.** Put it about 3 m up and 2 m in front of you, tilted down at about 45–50°, so your legs and shoes show. Not straight down. Use the 1× lens, not the ultra-wide.
- **Room around you.** Stand in the middle, with about 20% free space on every side. Keep your arms inside the frame.
- **Stay on one spot.** Move your arms and face, not your feet.
- **3 to 8 seconds.** Say your line to the lens.
- **Good light, plain floor.** Even light and a floor with no pattern look best.

## Good take, bad take

- **Film it like this.** Your whole body stands on the words. Legs and shoes in the shot, room on every side.
- **Not from straight above.** Your legs and feet go missing. From straight above they hide under your body. Film from further back.
- **Too close.** Part of you leaves the frame, and it ends in a straight cut. Leave room on every side.
- **Walking.** Your feet move, so the words don't stay lined up with you. Stand on one spot.
- **Two people.** floored works best with just you.

The studio's home screen shows a picture of each.

## Good to know

- The clip should be tall (9:16) and filmed with the phone upright. Wide videos get cropped.
- Up to 10 seconds works, with a warning. Shorter clips are quicker to process and look best.
- One person, one still camera. floored cuts you out of the video and puts the words on the floor around you.
- The studio shows a note about every problem it finds, in plain words, with what to change next time.
